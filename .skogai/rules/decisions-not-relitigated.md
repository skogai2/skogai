description: Check DECISIONS.md before treating a question as open
priority: 0
---
- Before proposing a choice on tooling, workflow, or architecture, check `DECISIONS.md` for whether
  it was already decided — don't relitigate a settled decision.
- When you do make or learn a new material decision, it belongs logged in `DECISIONS.md` (or as an
  open question there if unresolved), not just left in conversation.
